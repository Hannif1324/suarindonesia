Reproducing Reader Preview Modal XSS

Steps to Reproduce:

  1. Open http://localhost:5473/admin/index.html
  2. Navigate to Berita & Artikel > Tulis Konten Baru
  3. In the Title input field (#input-article-title), enter: 
    <img src="x" onerror="alert('STRIX-POC-XSS-TITLE-TRIGGERED')">
  4. Click Pratinjau Pembaca (#btn-reader-preview)
  5. Result: The alert box pops up immediately because title is injected into previewBody.innerHTML without escaping or DOMPurify.
